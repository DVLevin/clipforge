# CLIPFORGE - TikTok / Reels Content Plan (5 Concepts)

> Scope: Sprint 4 (Stage 15). Five video concepts with hook + visual description.
> Author: Growth (FACT-23). Final scripts/captions = Copywriter.

---

## 0. Why video matters here (the core insight)

CLIPFORGE is a VISUAL tool. The single highest-performing content pattern on TikTok/Reels for
utility and photo apps is the BEFORE -> AFTER TRANSFORMATION. Data from an Apr-2026 analysis of
3,600 TikTok videos across 6 niches (thecontentlabs.app) found the "visual proof / transformation"
hook outperforms every other pattern for utility products. People don't believe "AI removes
backgrounds" - they believe seeing a messy photo become a clean cutout in 2 seconds.

Three rules that govern all 5 concepts:
1. SHOW THE RESULT IN THE FIRST 1.5 SECONDS. The hook frame must already be the "wow" shot. Do not build up to it.
2. NATIVE, NOT POLISHED. Phone-shot, vertical, real hands, real screen recordings. Over-produced ads get swiped. Rough = authentic = trusted.
3. ONE IDEA PER VIDEO. "Remove background" OR "erase object" - never both. Split-brain videos underperform.

Platform note: TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts share the same algorithmic logic for
this category. Shoot once, post to all three. Optimal length: 7-15 seconds for pure wow;
20-35 seconds for tutorial/story.

## Concept 1 - "The Ex Eraser" (humor, highest viral ceiling)

**Hook (first 1.5s):** Split-screen. Left: a cute couple selfie. Right: same photo, the partner
GONE, replaced by clean background - seamless. On-screen text: "POV: you broke up"

**Visual description (3-7s):**
- 0.0s: freeze on the couple photo, finger hovers over the ex.
- 0.3s: finger brushes over the ex (screen recording, real gesture).
- 0.6s: 0.5-second "processing" shimmer.
- 0.9s: SNAP - ex is gone, background reconstructed flawlessly.
- 1.2s-3s: slow zoom on the clean result. Text: "it took 2 seconds."
- 3s-5s: quick cut to "also works on photobombers, exes, trash cans" - 3 rapid before/afters.

**Audio:** trending comedic audio OR the app's own "snap/whoosh" SFX + a trending instrumental.
Caption with a question to drive comments: "what would YOU erase first?"

**Why it works:** breakups are universal + emotional + shareable. The "erase your ex" framing
makes the tool the punchline of a relatable joke. High comment potential = algorithm boost.

**Call to action:** soft - app name watermark bottom-corner, link in bio. Do NOT hard-sell.

**Variants:** "erase the photobomber", "erase the trash can ruining your aesthetic shot",
"erase your sibling from the family photo".

## Concept 2 - "Product Photo in 5 Seconds" (ecom/creator niche, high INTENT)

**Hook (first 1.5s):** A cluttered desk with a product (sneaker / candle / jewelry) shot on a
messy surface. Text overlay: "your product photo is right here" with arrow. Then BAM - the
background dissolves into pure white studio backdrop.

**Visual description (5-8s):**
- 0.0s: messy real-world product shot (kitchen table, bad lighting).
- 0.3s: one tap - background highlights green (selected).
- 0.6s: tap "remove" - background gone, transparent checkerboard.
- 1.0s: tap "white bg" - clean studio white drops in.
- 1.3s: before/after slider wipe left-to-right.
- 2s-4s: montage of 4 products transformed the same way (sneaker, ring, candle, plushie).
- 4s-6s: text "sell more with clean photos" + small logo.

**Audio:** upbeat trending commerce audio (the "cha-ching" / money sound trends work here).
Caption: "etsy/poshmark/instagram sellers - this saves me 20 min per shoot."

**Why it works:** this is the HIGHEST-INTENT audience. Ecom sellers feel the pain daily and the
ROI is concrete (time = money). This video converts to installs, not just views. Tag
#etsy #poshmark #smallbusiness #productphotography #ecommerce.

**Call to action:** "link in bio - free to try." This is the one concept where a direct CTA is OK
because the audience is already commercial-minded.

## Concept 3 - "How Professionals Fake It" (educational, builds authority)

**Hook (first 1.5s):** Text on a boring talking-head frame: "Photoshop pros don't want you to
know this takes 2 seconds." OR "everyone thinks this needs Photoshop."

**Visual description (15-25s, tutorial pace):**
- 0-2s: the hook line, person looks at camera skeptically.
- 2-4s: "you want to remove this person from your vacation photo" - show a ruined beach photo with a stranger in it.
- 4-7s: "in Photoshop that's 10 minutes with the clone stamp" - quick boring Photoshop clip.
- 7-9s: "or..." - cut to phone, open CLIPFORGE.
- 9-12s: brush over the stranger, tap erase. 2 seconds. Gone.
- 12-15s: zoom on the reconstructed sand/water. "flawless."
- 15-18s: "this works on ANY object" - 2 more quick examples (power line in a landscape, trash can).
- 18-20s: "save this for later" + soft logo.

**Audio:** person's own voice (voiceover tutorials outperform silent ones for education content).
Caption: a real question -> "what's the hardest thing to remove from a photo?"

**Why it works:** the "insider secret" frame + comparison to the painful default (Photoshop)
creates the aha-moment. "Save this for later" drives saves, which TikTok weights heavily.

**Call to action:** none in-video. Goal is saves + shares + profile visits.

## Concept 4 - "The Impossible Erase" (challenge/stress-test, shareable)

**Hook (first 1.5s):** A genuinely hard photo - someone standing behind a complex fence, or a
person in front of a patterned wallpaper. Text: "AI can't remove THIS... right?"

**Visual description (6-10s):**
- 0.0s: the impossibly complex photo (fence + person + busy background).
- 0.5s: "watch" - brush over the person.
- 1.0s: process. The result reconstructs the fence AND the background behind where the person was.
- 2.0s: hold on the result. Slow pan. "okay what."
- 3s-5s: 3 more "impossible" erases in rapid succession (reflection in a mirror, shadow on grass, logo on a textured wall).
- 5s-7s: "drop a photo in the comments you think AI can't fix."

**Audio:** the "ohhh" / mind-blown trending audio, or dramatic beat drop synced to the reveal.

**Why it works:** sets up an expectation ("this can't be done") then demolishes it. The
pattern-break is what makes people rewatch and share. Comment-bait ("drop a photo") drives
engagement through the roof - and if you actually reply with edited versions, that's free UGC.

**Call to action:** "challenge us in the comments." Zero hard sell.

**Risk note:** only use this concept if the object eraser ACTUALLY performs well on complex
scenes. If it artifacts, do not post - it will get roasted in comments. Test internally first.

## Concept 5 - "Day in My Life as a Content Creator" (lifestyle/UGC, broad reach)

**Hook (first 1.5s):** Fast montage opener - "things in my phone that make me look way more
put-together than I am" - this is a proven TikTok format.

**Visual description (15-20s, fast cuts):**
- 0-1s: hook text.
- 1-3s: "thing 1: this app fixes my photos in 2 sec" - quick bg-remove demo on a selfie.
- 3-5s: "thing 2: ..." - (could be a different feature or keep it CLIPFORGE-only with a different use: erasing a stain on a shirt in a photo).
- 5-12s: weave 2-3 CLIPFORGE use-cases into a "my secrets" list - selfie cleanup, product shot, erase ex, fix a photobomb.
- 12-15s: "all from my phone, all free to try."
- 15s: logo + "follow for more creator hacks."

**Audio:** trending lifestyle vlog audio. Voiceover optional but helps.

**Why it works:** rides an established trend format ("things in my phone"), positions CLIPFORGE as
one tool among a creator's kit (less salesy), and reaches the broad "aspiring creator" audience
rather than just power users.

**Call to action:** "follow for more" - the goal here is follower growth, not direct installs.

## Production + posting cadence

- Batch-shoot all 5 in one session (2-3 hours). Use real phone screen recordings; no studio.
- Post cadence: 3-4 videos/week across TikTok + Reels + Shorts (same video, 3 platforms).
- Post timing: 11am-1pm and 7pm-9pm (local time of target market). Spain-first => CET.
- First 30 days: lead with Concept 1 (humor, broad reach) and Concept 2 (ecom, high intent).
  Hold Concept 4 (stress-test) until you have CONFIRMED the eraser handles complex scenes well.
- Hashtag strategy: 3-5 tags max. Mix one broad (#fyp, #photoedit), one niche
  (#backgroundremover, #productphotography), one community (#smallbusinesstiktok, #creatortok).

## Measurement (per video)
- Views, watch-time (completion rate > 50 percent = good), shares (highest-value signal),
  profile visits, link clicks.
- Install attribution: unique link per platform in bio (ref=tiktok, ref=reels, ref=shorts).
- Kill signal: a concept that gets under 500 views across 3 posts with good hooks -> retire it.
- Double-down signal: a concept hits 10k+ views -> make 3 variants of THAT hook immediately.

## Open questions (block before shooting)
1. Does CLIPFORGE actually erase complex scenes well (Concept 4)? Test before promising it.
2. Free tier limits? Concept 2's "free to try" CTA needs to be accurate.
3. Any watermark on exported images? If yes, it kills the "clean result" demos - must remove for
   pro or the before/after visuals look bad.
