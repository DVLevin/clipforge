## Format Notes

- **Recommended:** Portrait 1080 x 1920 px (mobile-first, fills more screen on phones)
- **Alternative:** Landscape 1920 x 1080 px (better for tablet display)
- **Max:** 8 screenshots allowed by Google Play — we use 5 (quality over quantity)
- **Layout per screenshot:** Bold headline at top (1/4), phone mockup or visual proof in center (2/4), sub-caption at bottom (1/4)

## Production Tips

### Phone Mockup Style
- Use a **dark phone frame** (matches our brand) — not the default white Apple mockup
- Phone screen content should use our dark theme UI
- Add a subtle Electric Teal glow around the phone frame
- Tilt phones slightly (5-10 degrees) for dynamic feel, OR keep flat for clarity

### Headline Typography
- Font: Space Grotesk Bold
- Size: 44-52px (readable even when Play Store shows thumbnails)
- Color: Off-White (`#F0F2F5`) with one word/phrase in an accent color
- Max 2 lines — if it doesn't fit, shorten it
- Center-aligned

### Background
- Solid Midnight Navy (`#0D0F1C`) for all 5 screenshots — consistency is key
- Optional: very subtle checkerboard texture at 2-3% opacity
- No gradients, no busy patterns — let the content breathe

### Before/After Photos
- Use **real, relatable photos** — a person in a messy room, a product on a cluttered desk, a beach with a photobomber
- Avoid stock-photo-perfect scenes — they feel fake
- The "after" should look genuinely impressive, not obviously edited
